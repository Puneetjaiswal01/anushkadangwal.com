Before You Become Anything,
Be Anushka.

♡

Dear Anushka,

Anushka, CAT is not everything. You are. You don't have to be the perfect daughter, the perfect student, or the strongest person in every room. You don't have to prove your worth through an exam, a percentile, a college, or a job. You just have to be Anushka. If you crack CAT, I'll celebrate you. If you don't, I'll still celebrate you. If you choose another path, I'll still be proud of you. I never fell in love with a degree, a college, or a future salary. I fell in love with you. I know I've made mistakes, and I'm sorry for the times I added pressure instead of peace. I want to listen better and show that through my actions. We share love, we share success, and we can share the difficult days too. Be ambitious, but don't forget to live. Chase your dreams, but never at the cost of yourself. The world will ask what you became. I hope you never forget who you are. Congratulations, meri Pahadan. Your Jaat ❤️
